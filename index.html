FAQs

Questions, answered.

How much will my phone repair cost?

Cost depends on your phone model, the cause of the issue and the parts required. Contact the shop for a quote after describing the fault or having the device assessed.

How long will a repair take?

Timing depends on the issue, parts availability and the work required. Please confirm the estimated time with the shop.

Should I back up my phone before repair?

Where possible, back up important data before handing over a device. Never share your OTPs, banking PINs or account passwords.

Can I ask about a repair before visiting?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to prepare a message with your phone model and the problem. WhatsApp sending requires the shop's real number to be configured in this file.
